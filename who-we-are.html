Our story

Why Mathatised exists

Mathatised eSports started with a simple frustration: plenty of players can compete at a high level, and very few of them get seen doing it. Results disappear into a bracket screenshot, and the performance disappears with them.

So we build the whole thing around the player. The format is designed to produce real series against real opposition. The broadcast exists so those series have an audience. The content exists so a standout performance lives longer than the match it happened in.

Tournaments are the vehicle. The players are the focus. The competition is the stage, the broadcast is the spotlight, and the community is what connects it all.

What we believe

Four principles we build on

Competition should mean something

A tournament should give players more than a bracket. Structure, standards and stakes are what make a result worth earning.

Players deserve a stage

Players should have the chance to demonstrate what they can do in front of an audience, not only in a private lobby.

High-level competition creates opportunity

Strong competition gives players the chance to test themselves and create moments people remember.

Esports is global

We're not built around one country or region. Players come to us from different countries, backgrounds and competitive levels.

Our global approach

Different regions. One competitive platform.

Our competitions are open to players from all over the world. Wherever you play from, if you're ready to compete, you're invited. No single country, no single region.
