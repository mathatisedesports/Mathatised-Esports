Ten matches decide it.

This is not a BO5 or a BO10. It's a two-leg format of five matches per leg, and every one of the ten matches is played.

Aggregate qualification

The outcome is decided on the combined result across all ten matches.

  1. Most match wins advancesThe team with more match wins across both legs goes through.
  2. Level on match wins? Goals decide.If the ten-match aggregate is tied, total goals scored across all ten matches is the deciding factor.

Illustrative example

Team A5 wins · 21 goalsAdvances
Team B5 wins · 18 goalsEliminated

Both teams win five matches, so Team A advances on more total goals scored across the ten matches.

Seeding

Balanced groups, competitive draw

Teams are seeded using the official MECS competitive seeding process. For the 32-team group stage, four pots of eight teams feed the draw, and every group receives one team from each pot.

The aim is balanced groups while keeping the draw genuinely competitive. Later stages are decided by results and the official MECS competition structure.
