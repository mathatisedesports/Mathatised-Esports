Player-first

Not just a tournament. A platform.

MECS is designed around the players in it. The point isn't another bracket to fill. It's a competitive stage where performances are visible.

Professional broadcasting, casting, production and social coverage are part of the series, so that competitive performances become moments people see rather than a line in a results table.

MECS is built for the community. The prize pool rewards the champions, but the real prize is the platform: the chance to test yourself against high-ranked, semi-pro and pro players, to compete with a full broadcast behind you, and to show the world what you're capable of.

The MECS standard

What every edition shares

Each edition runs on the same foundations, so a MECS title means the same thing wherever it's won.

Group Stage

24 teams in 8 groups of 3, single round-robin, every series BO3. Group winners go straight to the Championship Bracket.

A path for second place

Second-place teams play a qualification stage for the last four places in the bracket. Each edition's page sets out its format.

12-team Championship Bracket

Double elimination, with series growing from BO3 to BO5 to BO7, and a bracket reset in the Grand Final.

Balanced seeding

Three seeding pots of eight teams, with one team from each pot in every group.

Produced broadcasts

Live streams with casting and production on official match days, so performances reach an audience.

A prize pool for every edition

$150 in every edition, rewarding the champions of each region.
