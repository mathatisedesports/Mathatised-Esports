Where to compete

Every MECS event is run to the same competitive standard. Choose one to see its format, schedule and how to enter.

Player-first

Not just a tournament. A platform.

MECS is designed around the players in it. The point isn't another bracket to fill. It's a competitive stage where performances are visible.

Professional broadcasting, casting, production and social coverage are part of every event, so that competitive performances become moments people see rather than a line in a results table.

  • Compete
  • Challenge high-level players
  • Demonstrate your skill
  • Perform under pressure
  • Gain exposure
  • Be recognized
  • Build a reputation
  • Make a name for yourself

MECS is built for the community. It's a platform: the chance to test yourself against high-ranked, semi-pro and pro players, to compete with a full broadcast behind you, to see your best plays shared as highlights across our channels, and to show the world what you're capable of.

The MECS standard

What every MECS event shares

Whatever the region or format, a MECS title means the same thing wherever it's won.

A published rulebook

One official Mathatised Esports rulebook covers every event, so everyone plays by the same rules.

Dedicated match admins

Lobbies, results and rulings are handled by our admins, so you can focus on playing.

Broadcast and shared

Live on YouTube, Twitch and Kick, with highlights and edits on YouTube Shorts, TikTok, Instagram and Facebook, so performances reach an audience.

Casting & commentary

Play-by-play and analysis that turn your series into moments people remember.

A title to your name

Every event crowns a MECS champion, from the MECS Middle East Champion to the MECS APAC I Champion.

Open across regions

The Regionals rotate between the Middle East, APAC and EU, with more regions on the way.
