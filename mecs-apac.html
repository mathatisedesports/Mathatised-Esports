Qualifiers

1 weekend

The 8 teams that finish 2nd in their groups are split into two Swiss-format groups of four. The top two in each group claim the last four places in the Championship Bracket.

  • 8 teams in Qualifier Groups A and B
  • Each team plays the other three in its group
  • All Qualifiers series are BO3
  • Group A plays Saturday, Group B plays Sunday

Championship Bracket

2 weekends

8 group winners and 4 teams from the Qualifiers enter a 12-team double-elimination bracket. A team must lose twice to be eliminated.

  • 12 teams, double elimination
  • Opening rounds BO3, quarterfinals BO5
  • Semifinals BO5, bracket finals BO7
  • Grand Final BO7, with a bracket reset

How a Qualifier group works

Each group is a Swiss-format stage of four teams.

  1. Three series for every teamEach team plays the other three teams in its group, and every series is BO3.
  2. Points set the standingsTeams earn points from their wins and losses, and the group table is decided by points.
  3. The top two go throughThe top two in each group take the remaining four places in the Championship Bracket.

Two paths in. One bracket out.

A team must lose twice to be eliminated from the double-elimination Championship Bracket. That holds in the Grand Final too: if the lower-bracket team wins the first BO7, a second BO7 decides the champion.

Seeding

Balanced groups, competitive draw

Teams are seeded using the official MECS competitive seeding process. For the 24-team group stage, three pots of eight teams feed the draw, and every group receives one team from each pot.

The aim is balanced groups while keeping the draw genuinely competitive. Later stages are decided by results and the official MECS competition structure.
