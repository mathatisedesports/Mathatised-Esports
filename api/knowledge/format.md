MATHATISED ESPORTS
Mathatised Esports Championship Series
Tournament Format & Competition Structure
This document outlines the official tournament format, competition structure, and progression pathway for the Mathatised Esports Championship Series (MECS), a competition operated by Mathatised Esports.
1. Tournament Overview
The Mathatised Esports Championship Series (MECS) is a competitive esports competition operated by
Mathatised Esports. MECS is designed to provide pl ayers with a professional platform to compete, demonstrate their ability, gain recognition, and compete alongside or against high-level players.
The tournament will feature 24 teams, competing over a four-weekend competition period, and will ultimately det ermine one MECS Champion. Registration opens ahead of the competition period.

2. Overall Timeline
The MECS competition period runs for four consecutive weekends, structured across three phases: the
Group Stage (1 weekend), the Qualifiers (1 weekend), and the Championship Bracket (2 weekends).
Registration ope ns ahead of the competition period.
Registration (Pre-Competition)
Registration window: 1 week (opens ahead of the competition period)
Registration will open before the competition period begins and remain open for one week. During this window, eligible te ams may register for MECS. Once registration closes, teams will be reviewed and confirmed before the group-stage draw and seeding process. To be eligible, all players must hold a competitive rank of Champion or above.

4. Group Stage Qualification & Elimination
At the conclusion of the Group Stage, each group will have a ranking from 1st through 3rd. The results of each group determine the tournament’s next stage.
1st Place — Direct Qualification
The team finishing 1st in e ach group will qualify directly for the 12-team Championship Bracket. This produces 8 Group Winners, who are directly qualified for the Championship Bracket and will skip the
Qualifiers entirely, proceeding straight to the final double-elimination stage.
2 nd Place — Qualifiers
The team finishing 2nd in each group will advance to the Qualifiers. This produces 8 teams entering the
Qualifiers, from which 4 teams will advance, competing for the remaining four places in the 12-team
Championship Bracket.
3rd Plac e — Elimination
The team finishing 3rd in each group will be eliminated from MECS. The 8 third-place teams will not advance to the Qualifiers or the Championship Bracket.
5. Qualifiers
Duration: 1 weekend
The Qualifiers will take place across one weekend. The 8 teams advancing from the Group Stage (the 2nd - place finishers) will compete for the remaining four places in the 12-team Championship Bracket.
The 8 teams will be divided into two groups of four team s: Qualifier Group A and Qualifier Group B.
Swiss Format
Each group will compete in a Swiss-format stage. Each of the four teams in a group will play each of the other three teams in that group, accumulating points throughout the round based on wins and lo sses.
Group standings are determined by the points accumulated. All Qualifiers series will be Best-of-3 (BO3).
Qualifiers — Weekend Schedule
Qualifier Group A will play on Saturday and Qualifier Group B will play on Sunday, so both groups are completed wit hin the same weekend.
Qualifiers Outcome
- The top 2 teams from each group (4 teams in total) advance to the Championship Bracket
- The bottom 2 teams from each group (4 teams in total) are eliminated
6. Championship Bracket
Duration: 2 weekends
Following the Group Stage and the Qualifiers, the final 12 teams will compete in a double-elimination
Championship Bracket. The 12 teams consist of the 8 Group Winners who qualified directly and the 4 teams that advanced from the Qualifiers (the top 2 team s from each Qualifier group).
The Championship Bracket will use a standard double-elimination structure: a team must lose twice to be eliminated.
There will be a bracket reset in the Grand Final. The Grand Final is a Best-of-7 (BO7) series. Because the
Upp er Bracket finalist enters undefeated while the Lower Bracket finalist enters with one loss, a Lower
Bracket win in the first series levels both teams at one loss each. A bracket reset means a second BO7 series is then played to decide the champion. This k eeps the double-elimination rule fair: the Upper
Bracket team must also lose twice to be eliminated.
7. Championship Match Formats
Match formats increase in length as the Championship Bracket progresses, reflecting the rising stakes of each round.
Stage M atch Format
Opening Rounds (leading up to Quarterfinals) Best-of-3 (BO3)
Upper Bracket & Lower Bracket Quarterfinals Best-of-5 (BO5)
Upper Bracket & Lower Bracket Semifinals Best-of-5 (BO5)
Bracket Finals & Grand Final Best-of-7 (BO7)
BO3 → BO5 → BO7
The Grand Final is a Best-of-7 (BO7) series, with a bracket reset if the Lower Bracket team wins the first series.
8. Seeding & Draw Structure
Initial Group Stage Seeding
For the initial 24-team Group Stage:
- Teams will be evaluated and seeded accord ing to MECS’s official competitive seeding process.
- The 24 teams will be divided into three seeding pots of eight teams.
- Each group will receive one team from each seeding pot.
- The purpose of the pot system is to create balanced and competitive groups whil e maintaining a fair draw.
- The exact internal criteria used by Mathatised Esports to determine seeding remain at the discretion of the tournament administration.
Subsequent Stage Seeding
For later stages, Qualifiers and Championship Bracket positions will be determined according to MECS's standard seeding process.
