# MECS Rules & Regulations (official rulebook, full text)

Source: /assets/docs/mecs-rules-and-regulations.pdf. Section numbers below are the official rule numbers. Note: section 1.2 of the PDF names the 'South Asia Premier League (MECS)'; this is a typo in the document and means the Mathatised Esports Championship Series (MECS).

MATHATISED ESPORTS
Mathatised Esports Championship Series
Rocket League Competitions — Official Rules & Regulations
These Rules establish the standing rules governing Mathatised Esports Championship Series (MECS)
Rocket League competitions operated by Mathatised Esports. Tournament-specific announcements supplement these Rules and govern the details of individual tourna ments.
1. General Provisions
1.1 — Purpose of the Rules
- These Rules establish the framework for fair competition, competitive integrity, consistent tournament administration, participant conduct, match procedures, enforcement, and tournament operations.
- All participants are expected to understand and comply with these Rules together with the applicable tournament-specific rules and announcements.
1.2 — Tournament Organizer
- Mathatised Esports is the Organizer and Operator of the South Asia Premier League (MECS) and its individual tournaments.
1.3 — Mathatised Esports Championship Series
- MECS is the overarching Rocket League esports competition and brand operated by Mathatised Esports.
- Individual MECS tournaments, including 1v1, 2v2, and 3v3 competitions, are independent competitions unless Mathatised expressly announces a standings, points, ranking, or seasonal system.
- Mathatised may introduce connected seasons, points, rankings, or other league structures in the future through applicable tournament announcements or rule amendments.
1.4 — Applicability
- These Rules apply to Players, Teams, Captains/Points of Contact, registered substitutes, coaches, manage rs, owners, and other persons participating in or directly associated with an MECS tournament where applicable.
- A violation by an associated person does not automatically result in punishment of the entire Team. Team responsibility is determined according to the circumstances and the applicable enforcement provisions.
1.5 — Tournament-Specific Rules
- These standing Rules govern MECS competitions generally. Each tournament may publish additional tournament-specific rules and information, including format, dates, schedule, server region, number of slots, rank or MMR requirements, bracket structure, regis tration deadline, prize structure, and other competition details.
- Where a tournament-specific rule conflicts with these standing Rules, the tournament-specific rule takes precedence for that tournament only.
1.6 — Administrator Authority
- Mathatised Esports and its authorized Tournament Administrators have final authority over the interpretation, administration, and enforcement of these Rules.
- Participants may submit a review request through the official process, but submitting a request does not suspend or automatically change an administrative decision.
1.7 — Rule Amendments
- Mathatised Esports may amend, update, or clarify these Rules when reasonably necessary.
- Material changes will generally not be applied retroactively to an ongoing tournament unless nece ssary because of exceptional circumstances, competitive integrity, or proper administration.
1.8 — Exceptional Circumstances and Administrative Discretion
- These Rules are enforced as written by default. However, an authorized Tournament Administrator may m ake an exception where a genuinely exceptional circumstance makes strict application clearly unreasonable or would materially harm fair competition or tournament operations.
- An exception is discretionary, does not create an automatic right for another part icipant, and does not establish a permanent precedent.
1.9 — Independent Competition
- MECS is independently operated by Mathatised Esports and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or officially connected to Epic Games, Psyonix, or the Rocket L eague Championship Series (RLCS), unless expressly stated otherwise.
2. Tournament Structure & Administration
2.1 — Tournament Format
- MECS may conduct 1v1, 2v2, 3v3, or other Rocket League formats.
- The exact format, match structure, series length, and com petition stages are announced separately for each tournament.
2.2 — Tournament Schedule
- Before registration closes, Mathatised will generally publish expected match-time windows to help Teams plan availability.
- Published windows are planning information an d are not a guarantee of an exact match time.
Actual scheduling may depend on tournament structure, progression, match duration, and tournament operations.
- Teams must communicate known availability limitations before the schedule is announced.
Mathatised w ill make reasonable efforts to accommodate such limitations but does not guarantee accommodation.
- Once the schedule is announced, Teams are expected to be available for their assigned matches.
- Day-of rescheduling requests are generally not entertained exce pt in genuinely exceptional circumstances.
- Failure to participate in a scheduled match may result in a forfeit. Teams should not expect matches to be moved outside the published tournament window solely for convenience.
2.3 — Bracket and Competition Struct ure
- The competition structure for each tournament is determined by Mathatised and announced before competition.
- Possible structures include, without limitation, single elimination, double elimination, round robin, Swiss, group stages, or other formats.
- Adv ancement and elimination procedures are tournament-specific.
- If both Teams fail or refuse to participate in a scheduled match, the Administrators will determine the appropriate result based on the tournament structure, the circumstances, competitive integrity, and tournament continuity. Where a temporary bracket pla cement is required, an Administrator may use a random determination or other reasonable method and may subsequently correct the bracket as required by the tournament format.
- Teams may not mutually arrange or intentionally cause a scheduled match not to be played. Such conduct may be treated as a competitive-integrity violation rather than an ordinary forfeit.
2.4 — Tournament Capacity and Slots
- Each tournament’s capacity and number of available slots will be announced separately.
- Capacity is normally fixed for that tournament. Mathatised may increase or decrease capacity where reasonably necessary and will communicate material changes where practicable.
2.5 — Registration
- Registration is generally first-come, first-served, provided that all applicable eligibility and tournament-specific requirements are satisfied.
- Registration must be completed before the announced deadline.
- A Team may register up to one substitute.
- Roster additions and changes are permitted until registration closes. Once registration closes, the roster is locked except where these Rules or an Administrator-approved exceptional circumstance permits otherwise.
- If a registered Team withdraws or fails to parti cipate before the tournament begins, Mathatised may reopen the vacated slot or offer it to another eligible Team or Player at its discretion.
2.6 — Check-In and Match Readiness
- Teams must acknowledge match notifications through the designated MECS Discord communication process and remain reasonably reachable.
- The Team POC is the primary contact and should remain responsive. Players should be present in the Team’s designated voice channel where applicable.
- Match credentials are normally provided before the s cheduled start time.
- By the end of the applicable grace period, the required number of eligible players must be ready:
1v1 requires one Player, 2v2 requires two Players, and 3v3 requires three Players.
- A registered substitute may replace an unavailable Sta rter in accordance with the roster rules.
- If a Team does not have the required number of eligible Players by the applicable deadline, the shorthanded Team may choose either to play the match shorthanded (for example, 2v3, 1v2, or 1v3) or to forfeit the mat ch. The opposing Team does not choose this outcome; the choice belongs to the shorthanded Team alone.
- Shorthanded play such as 2v3, 1v2, or 1v3 is optional, not required. If the shorthanded Team declines to play, the match is forfeited in accordance with t hese Rules.
- Players may not enter an active game on their own initiative. A Player or substitute arriving after a game begins may only participate from a subsequent game, subject to the roster rules. The only exception is a Player disconnect handled under Section 4.7, during which a registered substitute may enter or the disconnected Player may rejoin.
- Normally, the grace period is 10 minutes from the official scheduled match time. If match credentials were posted before the scheduled time, the deadline rem ains scheduled time plus 10 minutes. If credentials are posted after the scheduled time because of an administrative delay, the grace period runs 10 minutes from credential posting.
- An Administrator may grant additional time in a genuinely exceptional circ umstance.
2.7 — Tournament Administration
- The current authorized Tournament Administrators are:
- Primary Tournament Administrator: Wali Hassan (Mathatised) — Discord: Mathatised.
- Secondary Tournament Administrator: Ali Hassan.
- No other person is currently a uthorized as an MECS Tournament Administrator.
- Only instructions and decisions issued by an authorized Tournament Administrator are official MECS administrative direction.
- Both authorized Administrators may administer matches, enforce Rules, manage brackets and results, handle disputes and match issues, communicate decisions, and exercise the discretion granted by these Rules.
- If Mathatised formally designates and announces an additional Tournament Administrator in the future, that person will have t he same authority while holding that designation.
2.8 — Changes to Tournament Structure
- Once announced, a tournament’s bracket and competition structure are generally locked.
- Mathatised may change the structure, schedule, advancement procedure, or other to urnament mechanics when exceptional circumstances make such action reasonably necessary to preserve fair competition, competitive integrity, or tournament continuity.
- Material changes will be communicated to affected Teams where reasonably possible. Teams do not have a general right to require structural changes.
3. Player & Team Eligibility
3.1 — Age Requirement
- Players must be at least 13 years old, or older where a higher minimum age is required by applicable local law.
3.2 — Epic / Rocket League Accoun t Requirements and Account History
- Players must compete using their main or primary Rocket League account unless an alternate account has been disclosed and expressly approved under Section 3.4.
- Alt, secondary, borrowed, shared, purchased, gifted, transfer red, or otherwise unauthorized accounts are prohibited unless expressly approved under the alternate-account exception.
- Mathatised may review account history where appropriate to verify eligibility and the legitimacy of a Player’s registered rank.
- The foll owing lifetime-win thresholds are screening baselines for additional review and are not automatic proof of an alternate account or misconduct:
- C1: 500 lifetime wins; C2: 750; C3: 1,000; GC1: 1,500; GC2: 2,000; GC3: 2,500; SSL: 3,000.
- Additional review may consider lifetime wins, ranked matches, season history, account age and progression, peak rank, consistency of account history, and other publicly available information.
- A Player below a screening threshold is subject to additional verification or review, not automatic rejection or a finding of cheating.
- Evidence that an account is an unauthorized alternate, purchased, shared, borrowed, transferred, or otherwise improperly used may result in rejection, removal, disqualification, or other discipline under th ese Rules.
- Discovery of an unauthorized account after competition has begun does not prevent Mathatised from taking action.
3.3 — Registration and Account Verification Information
- Each registered Player must provide: Discord ID, Epic Games ID, and a Rocket League Tracker profile link.
- The Rocket League Tracker profile link is mandatory and serves as the primary account-verification reference. The Epic Games ID remains required.
- A Team may optionally submit a Team logo. Mathatised may provide or create a bas ic Team graphic where no logo is supplied.
- Players and Teams may optionally provide background, origin, playstyle, tactics, achievements, or other profile information for broadcast and promotional use. Such information is not itself an eligibility requirem ent unless expressly requested for verification.
- For 1v1, the Player submits their own information. For 2v2 and 3v3, the designated Team representative submits the required information for all registered Players.
- Providing the required information does not guarantee acceptance or eligibility.
3.4 — Main and Alternate Account Policy
- The main or primary Rocket League account is required unless an alternate account is expressly approved before the Player competes.
- An alternate account may be approved only when the Player discloses it before registration closes, provides the main account, gives a legitimate reason, provides reasonable supporting evidence where requested, and receives explicit approval from an authori zed Tournament Administrator before competing.
- Providing an alternate account without explicit approval does not constitute authorization.
- Where an alternate account is approved, the main account remains the reference for seeding, rank/MMR, eligibility, an d other applicable processes. The registration should identify that the Player is competing on an approved alternate account.
- An undisclosed or unapproved alternate account remains prohibited and may result in disqualification or further discipline.
3.5 — Player and Team Names
- Player and Team names must be appropriate for a professional esports broadcast and must comply with the Code of Conduct.
- Offensive, discriminatory, sexually explicit, excessively vulgar, or otherwise inappropriate names are prohibited .
- Names may not misleadingly impersonate another Player, Team, Administrator, organization, public figure, or Mathatised representative.
- Names may not improperly use or imply affiliation with Epic Games, Psyonix, Rocket League, RLCS, or Mathatised Esports.
- Mathatised may require a Player or Team to change a name at any time where reasonably necessary. Refusal to comply may result in removal from competition.
- Names should remain consistent throughout a tournament unless an Administrator approves a change.
3. 6 — Team Roster and Captain / Point of Contact
- Roster limits are: 1v1 — one Starter plus up to one optional substitute; 2v2 — two Starters plus up to one optional substitute; 3v3 — three Starters plus up to one optional substitute.
- A substitute is optional and there may be no more than one registered substitute per Team.
- Each Team must designate one registered Player as its Captain / Point of Contact (POC) at registration.
- The POC is the Team’s primary administrative and communication contact and is respons ible for submitting Team information and handling official communications with Administrators.
- In 1v1, the Player is automatically their own POC. In 2v2 and 3v3, one registered Player must be designated as POC.
- The POC may be changed before registration cl oses. After roster lock, a POC change requires Administrator approval.
3.7 — Roster Changes, Substitutions and Roster Lock
- Roster changes are permitted until registration closes.
- After registration closes, the roster is locked. Post-lock changes are genera lly prohibited unless an exceptional circumstance is approved by an Administrator.
- A registered substitute may replace a Starter after roster lock without separate approval, provided the Team notifies an Administrator before the match so that match adminis tration and opponent communication can be handled correctly.
- Substitution normally occurs between games in a series. The only exception is a Player disconnect handled under Section 4.7, where a registered substitute may enter the game during the pause.
- If no registered substitute is available and a required Starter cannot participate, the Team may choose to play shorthanded in accordance with Section 2.6, or the match will be forfeited if the Team elects not to play shorthanded.
- An Administrator may approve an exceptional roster change where circumstances genuinely warrant it. Such approval is discretionary and is not a general method for bypassing the roster lock.
3.8 — Player Exclusivity
- A Player may be registered to or compete for only one Team in the sam e tournament.
- This applies equally to Starters and substitutes.
- A Player may not compete for one Team while serving as a registered substitute for another Team in the same tournament.
3.9 — Multiple Teams and Organizational Associations
- The same organization, owner, manager, or other associated person may be involved with multiple Teams, provided each Team has a completely distinct Player roster.
- No Player may overlap between such Teams, including as Starter, substitute, Captain/POC, or other regi stered Player role.
- Teams may not use organizational relationships to circumvent Player exclusivity or other eligibility requirements.
3.10 — Team Eligibility and Verification
- Every Team and Player must satisfy these Rules and all tournament-specific requi rements.
- Mathatised may verify eligibility at registration or at any later point.
- Players and Teams must provide accurate information and reasonably cooperate with verification.
- Verification may include account history, rank/MMR, roster information, regist ration details, and other relevant information.
- If ineligibility is discovered after registration or competition, an Administrator will review the circumstances and may apply an appropriate permitted consequence.
- Possible consequences include a warning, ga me loss, match loss/forfeit, removal of a Player, Team disqualification, loss of prize eligibility, or another penalty expressly permitted by these Rules.
- An individual violation does not automatically penalize the entire Team; however, serious violations involving the Team or materially affecting competitive integrity may justify Team-level action.
3.11 — Ownership, Management and Conflicts
- The same individual or organization may own, manage, or coach multiple Teams where otherwise permitted, provided the Teams maintain distinct rosters and comply with these Rules.
- Multiple organizational associations are not inherently prohibited.
- Collusion, match manipulation, sharing confidential competitive information, unfair coordination, and attempts to obtain an adv antage through multiple-Team relationships are prohibited.
- Teams and associated persons may not circumvent these restrictions through another person, account, or organizational arrangement.
- Administrators may investigate suspected conflicts or improper coo rdination and take action under the enforcement provisions.
3.12 — Registration Submission and Confirmation
- Registration is submitted through the designated MECS Discord registration process.
- The Captain/POC is responsible for submitting the required Team and Player information.
- Registration submission does not itself guarantee acceptance.
- Mathatised reviews registrations for eligibility and applicable tournament requirements.
- Once a Team has been reviewed and accepted, Mathatised will add it to the designated Registered channel. Being added to the Registered channel means the Team is officially confirmed as registered.
- Registration confirmation may later be reversed if new information establishes ineligibility, inaccurate information, unauthor ized account use, or a rule or integrity violation.
4. Gameplay & Match Procedures
4.1 — Match Settings
- Unless a tournament-specific rule states otherwise, MECS matches use standard Rocket League competitive settings.
- Standard settings include Rocket League, Standard mode, 5-minute games, no bots, no mutators, private matches, and a standard competitive arena designated by Mathatised.
- Match credentials are provided through the designated MECS process.
- Team size and serv er region are determined by the applicable tournament-specific rules.
- Players must use supported Rocket League platforms and normal competitive gameplay functionality.
- Any tournament-specific setting takes precedence for that tournament.
4.2 — Match Hostin g, Server and Team Colors
- The Match Administrator creates and hosts the private match lobby unless the tournament - specific procedure states otherwise.
- Match credentials are provided through the designated MECS process.
- There is no fixed standing Blue/Orang e assignment; team colors are determined by the game/lobby unless a tournament-specific instruction says otherwise.
- For a Middle East tournament, matches are played on ME4 unless an approved exception applies.
- If an incorrect server is used, such as ME6 in stead of ME4, the match must be recreated on the correct server. New credentials are provided and Players must leave and rejoin the corrected lobby.
- Teams should notify the Administrator promptly when an incorrect server or material lobby issue is identifi ed.
4.3 — Match Start and Player Readiness
- The Match Administrator waits for the required Players to enter the lobby.
- The Administrator may ask both Teams to confirm readiness before starting the game.
- Once all required Players have confirmed readiness, th e Administrator starts the match.
- The normal Rocket League in-game countdown is the game start procedure.
- A Player arriving within the applicable grace period may participate, subject to the roster rules.
- If a Player disconnects before a match begins, the Administrator may wait a reasonable short period. If the issue continues, an eligible registered substitute may be used. If the required roster cannot be fielded, a forfeit may apply.
- Additional time may be granted only where an Administrator determines th at an exceptional circumstance justifies it.
4.4 — Match Results and Bracket Updates
- The Match Administrator records the official game and series result and updates the bracket or applicable tournament record.
- Teams must remain available to confirm or reso lve material discrepancies.
- Administrators may request screenshots, replays, recordings, or other evidence where a result is disputed or questioned.
- False, altered, fabricated, or misleading match information may result in disciplinary action.
4.5 — Match Lobby Access and Observers
- Only authorized persons may join an MECS match lobby: participating Players, registered substitutes, coaches associated with participating Teams, authorized MECS casters, and authorized Tournament Administrators.
- Friends, spectators, unregistered Team members, and other unauthorized observers may not join.
- Teams may not invite unauthorized persons even if both Teams agree.
- An Administrator may prevent or remove an unauthorized participant.
4.6 — Server Region and Server Sel ection
- The tournament announcement designates the server region.
- The designated region normally remains fixed throughout the tournament.
- Where the designated region is Middle East, ME4 is the required server unless an approved exception applies.
- A differen t server region may be used only when both Teams agree and an authorized Tournament Administrator approves the change before the match is played on that region.
- Approved server exceptions may be recorded or communicated by the Administrator.
4.7 — Disconne cts and Technical Issues
- Individual Player Disconnect: If a Player disconnects, the game will be paused by the Match Administrator and a two-minute timer starts.
- If the disconnected Player returns before the timer ends, the Player may rejoin and the game r esumes with the full roster.
- If the Player has not returned, a registered substitute may enter the game during the pause, subject to the roster and substitution rules.
- If the Player has not returned and no registered substitute is available, the affected Team continues the game shorthanded once the two-minute timer ends.
- The pause will not be extended beyond two minutes. When the timer ends, the game resumes whether or n ot the disconnected Player has returned.
- A Player who returns after the two-minute timer has ended may still rejoin the game upon returning, unless a registered substitute has already entered; the game will not be paused again to wait for that Player.
- If t he game ends while a Player is disconnected, the Player may rejoin from the next game of the series, or a registered substitute may participate in the next game.
- If the original Player returns while a substitute is playing, the original Player must wait un til that game ends before replacing the substitute.
- Repeated disconnects are handled under the same principles. Teams are responsible for maintaining a stable connection and eligible roster, subject to Administrator discretion in exceptional circumstances.
- Full Lobby / Server Disconnect: If all Players are disconnected from the match, the Administrator records the game state at the moment of the full disconnect, including time, score, and other relevant information.
- A new lobby may then be created and the g ame resumed from the established game time and score. Goals scored before the full disconnect remain valid; scoring after the disconnect and before the authorized resumption does not count.
- Administrator Disconnect: If the Match Administrator disconnects, the game continues without an automatic pause. The Administrator will attempt to reconnect promptly.
- If necessary, the Administrators may designate another authorized person to temporarily monitor or administer the match.
- If no replacement Administrator ca n join, Teams must truthfully report the relevant game and series results to the Administrator at the end of the game or series, and the result will be verified and recorded as soon as practicable.
- Individual Connection Problems: A Player is responsible fo r maintaining a stable connection. Poor connection quality that does not cause a disconnect does not automatically stop or restart a game. No restart is required solely because of one Player’s unstable connection.
4.8 — Pauses and Team Timeouts
- Players and Teams may not request or initiate pauses during an active game. Pauses are only allowed in situations such as a Player disconnect under Section 4.7.
- Only a Match Administrator may pause a game, and only for a disconnect under Section 4.7 or where necessar y for administrative, technical, or exceptional reasons.
- Each Team receives one two-minute timeout per series.
- A Team may request its timeout only between completed games, after a game has ended and before the next game begins.
- Timeouts do not carry over to another series.
- The Administrator controls when play resumes after a timeout.
4.9 — Forfeits and Match Conces sions
- A Team may not voluntarily forfeit an active series merely because it does not wish to continue.
- A Team may not intentionally concede while it remains able to field its required roster, regardless of the current score.
- If a genuine circumstance makes continuation impossible, such as a Team losing a required Player and having no eligible substitute, the Team may notify an Administrator. The Administrator may award the remaining match or series to the opponent.
- Deliberate or unnecessary concessions inte nded to manipulate competition may be treated as a competitive-integrity violation.
4.10 — Game and Series Abandonment
- Players and Teams are expected to continue a match unless a legitimate circumstance prevents continuation.
- Intentional stopping, refusal to continue, deliberate throwing or giving of games or series, or leaving a match lobby without legitimate reason is prohibited.
- Players may not pause an active game to discuss or request a concession. The game continues under the normal procedures.
- Techni cal problems should be reported promptly, but a technical complaint does not automatically pause the game.
- Leaving a lobby without authorization or a legitimate explanation may be treated as abandonment.
- Intentional abandonment or refusal to continue may r esult in disqualification from the current tournament and/or a future MECS suspension or ban, subject to the available penalties and circumstances.
- Genuine inability to continue is handled under the applicable roster and forfeit provisions and is not autom atically misconduct.
4.11 — Match Replays and Evidence
- Teams are not generally required to save or retain replays.
- Where a dispute, complaint, or competitive-integrity concern arises, an Administrator may request replays, screenshots, videos, recordings, o r other relevant evidence.
- Participants making a claim should provide supporting evidence as soon as reasonably possible.
- Where evidence is necessary to resolve a current series, the Administrator may specify a submission period, normally around 30 minutes or another period appropriate to the circumstances.
- Participants must cooperate with reasonable evidence requests.
- Fake, altered, fabricated, manipulated, or deliberately misleading evidence is prohibited.
- Refusal to reasonably cooperate with an evidence request may be considered in enforcement.
4.12 — Match Communication and Administrator Instructions
- After registration, each Team is provided a designated communication channel within the official MECS Discord.
- That Team channel is the primary operational channel for match information, credentials, schedule information, and Administrator instructions.
- The POC is the primary communication contact. If unavailable, an Administrator may communicate with another registered Player or appropriate Team representative.
- Teams should remain reasonably reachable and attentive in the designated Discord voice/channel environment during their scheduled match window.
- Teams and Players must follow reasonable Administrator instructions concerning lobbies, servers, match procedures, evidence, and tournament operations.
- Deliberately ignoring or refusing a reasonable Administrator instruction may result in disciplinary action.
4.13 — Coaches, Substitutes and Team Representatives
- A registered substitute and a registere d Team coach may join the designated Team Discord voice channel and communicate with active Players during games and between games.
- Coach and substitute communication must occur through the designated Discord voice channel or another authorized communication method, not through prohibited or exposed means.
- Active Players may use normal Rocket League in-game communication and Quick Chat.
- An authorized coach or registered substitute may spectate the Team’s match from the lobby where the tournament setup permits it.
- A registered substitute may replace an active Player between games, or during a disconnect pause under Section 4.7, subject to the roster and substitution rules.
- Only active Players, registered substitutes, registered coaches, authorized Tournament Administrators, and authorized MECS casters may use match-lobby credentials.
- Teams must not share match credentials with unauthorized persons.
4.14 — Coaching and Outside Assistance
- A registered coach may provide tactical, strategic, and technic al instructions to the Team during an active game.
- A registered substitute may provide the same type of Team assistance.
- Coach and substitute communication must occur through the designated Team Discord voice channel.
- Normal Rocket League Quick Chat remain s available to active Players.
- Authorized coaches and substitutes may use legitimate observation information for live instruction.
- Replay analysis and legitimate analytical tools are permitted where they do not provide prohibited real-time competitive assi stance.
- No person outside the registered Team or otherwise authorized MECS personnel may provide live information, coaching, tactical instructions, or competitive assistance directly to a Team during a match.
- Public livestream chat or general spectator com mentary is not treated as authorized Team communication; this rule concerns direct intervention or deliberate communication with the Team.
- Unauthorized outside assistance or attempts to circumvent this rule may result in discipline.
4.15 — Game Bugs, Explo its and Unauthorized Modifications
- Players may not intentionally exploit bugs, glitches, or unintended mechanics to obtain a competitive advantage.
- Unauthorized modification of the Rocket League client, game files, or tournament environment is prohibited.
- Macros, automated inputs, scripts, cheats, or software or hardware that provides an unfair competitive advantage are prohibited.
- Third-party tools or modifications may not be used to provide prohibited assistance or alter normal competitive gameplay.
- Bakke sMod and similar tools are not prohibited merely by name; legitimate cosmetic or analytical functionality is not automatically a violation, but any use that provides an unfair competitive advantage or prohibited assistance is prohibited.
- Intentionally discovering, using, sharing, or continuing to exploit a known competitive exploit may result in discipline.
- Administrators may investigate suspected exploits and request relevant evidence.
4.16 — Account Sharing and Player Identity
- Only the r egistered Player may play on the registered Rocket League/Epic account.
- Allowing another person to play on a registered account is prohibited, even with the account holder’s permission.
- Using another person’s account is prohibited.
- An Administrator may req uest reasonable account or identity verification where a legitimate concern exists, and the Player must cooperate.
- Discovery of account sharing or identity misuse after a match does not prevent disciplinary action.
- Action may apply to the individual Player , the Team, or both depending on the circumstances.
- Approved alternate accounts are governed exclusively by Section 3.4.
4.17 — Match Integrity and Collusion
- Match fixing, collusion, intentional result manipulation, deliberate throwing, coordinated outcome s, bribery or benefits intended to influence a match, sharing confidential competitive information for unfair advantage, coordinated mutual no-shows, and deliberate attempts to undermine a legitimate competitive outcome are prohibited.
- Administrators may i nvestigate suspected integrity violations and apply the permitted disciplinary measures based on severity, intent, competitive impact, and other circumstances.
5. Match Issues, Disputes & Records
5.1 — Technical Issues
- Players and Teams may report technic al problems to an Administrator through the official support process.
- Teams do not need the opposing Team’s agreement to report a technical issue.
- A one-Player disconnect is handled under Section 4.7.
- A technical complaint does not automatically pause, res tart, or replay a game.
- An Administrator may review the issue after the game or series and determine the appropriate action.
- Where a server-wide or full-lobby failure materially affects the game, the Administrator may apply the full-disconnect procedure or another remedy permitted by these Rules.
5.2 — Administrative Intervention and Emergency Action
- An Administrator may intervene during an active game or series when necessary for proper administration, competitive integrity, participant safety, or another exceptional circumstance.
- An Administrator may pause, continue, stop, or otherwise direct the match where permitted by these Rules.
- Players and Teams must follow the Administrator’s instructions.
- Any resulting disciplinary action must be within the penalti es and authority provided by these Rules.
5.3 — Match Disputes and Challenges
- Formal disputes and challenges must be submitted through the official Mathatised Esports Discord ticket system.
- The Captain/POC should normally raise a dispute. If the POC is unavailable, another registered Player or authorized Team representative may do so.
- Outsiders do not have authority to file a dispute on behalf of a Team unless an Administrator permits it.
- Disputes should be raised immediately or as soon as reasonably pos sible after the relevant event.
- Participants should report a suspected violation or tournament issue directly to Mathatised before circulating the matter to friends, other Teams, public communities, or other unauthorized individuals.
- Only the authorized To urnament Administrators — currently Wali Hassan (Mathatised) and Ali Hassan — are the designated people to whom such matters should first be reported.
- If an Administrator determines that a participant deliberately shared or circulated the information with unauthorized persons before reporting it to Mathatised, Mathatised may reject, decline to consider, or nullify the claim.
- Good-faith reporting and legitimate evidence submission to an Administrator are not prohibited by this requirement.
- Submitting a dispu te does not automatically pause a tournament or bracket. Unless an Administrator instructs otherwise, competition continues.
- Teams should not publicly circulate a dispute or attempt to resolve it through other Teams or public channels before bringing it to Mathatised.
- Knowingly false, misleading, or bad-faith disputes may result in discipline under the applicable Rules.
- Administrative decisions are subject to the finality provisions in Section 12.
5.4 — Match Result Finality
- The Match Administrator’s record ed result and bracket update constitute the official tournament record.
- Once a result has been recorded and the bracket has advanced, the result is normally final.
- An Administrator may correct or change a recorded result where new material information, a R ules violation, a competitive-integrity issue, an administrative error, or an exceptional circumstance requires corrective action.
- Possible corrective action may include reversal of a result, Team disqualification, bracket adjustment, replay, or another ac tion expressly permitted by these Rules.
- Teams do not have a general right to reopen a result merely because they disagree with it.
5.5 — Official Tournament Records
- Official tournament records include brackets and results maintained by Mathatised, officia l announcements, registration and roster records, and administrative decisions communicated through official MECS channels, including designated private Team channels and tickets.
- Third-party claims, private conversations outside official channels, and scr eenshots of unofficial statements are not themselves official tournament records.
- Administrators may nevertheless consider unofficial information as supporting evidence where it is relevant and credible.
6. Communication & Support
6.1 — Official Support a nd Contact Channels
- The official support method for MECS is the Mathatised Esports Discord ticket system.

7. Code of Conduct & Competitive Integrity
7.1 — General Conduct
- Participants must conduct themselves professionally and respectfully throughout MECS.
- Normal competitive banter and trash talk are permitt ed when they do not become harassment, threats, discrimination, targeted abuse, intimidation, or deliberate disruption.
- Harassment, threats, discrimination, targeted abuse, intimidation, and deliberate disruption of tournament operations or official channe ls are prohibited.
- Impersonating a Player, Team, Administrator, or Mathatised representative is prohibited.
- Conduct outside the tournament may be considered where it is directly connected to MECS or materially affects participants, tournament operations, o r competitive integrity.
7.2 — Harassment, Discrimination and Personal Attacks
- Hate speech, slurs, or discriminatory conduct targeting protected characteristics are prohibited.
- Sexual harassment or sexually abusive conduct is prohibited.
- Threats of violenc e, physical harm, or credible unlawful retaliation are prohibited.
- Doxxing or sharing another person’s private information without authorization is prohibited.
- Encouraging, organizing, or directing others to harass, threaten, or target a participant is pro hibited.
- Repeated unwanted contact or targeted abuse is prohibited.
- Sharing private conversations, images, recordings, or personal material with the intent to harass, embarrass, threaten, or target another participant is prohibited.
- Retaliation or intimidation against a person for reporting a violation or cooperating with an investigation is prohibited.
7.3 — Wagering and Gambling
- Players, substitutes, coaches, Team representatives, and other participants may not bet or wager on MECS matches or tour naments.
- Participants may not arrange, facilitate, promote, encourage, or profit from wagers connected to MECS.
- Participants may not use tournament information, access, relationships, or position to obtain a gambling-related advantage.
- Circumvention throug h another person or account is also prohibited.
7.4 — Fraud, Deception and Misrepresentation
- Participants must provide accurate and truthful information to Mathatised and its Administrators.
- Knowingly false, misleading, deceptive, or materially incomplete information is prohibited.
- This applies to registration, identity, roster information, rank o r eligibility information, match claims, evidence, administrative communications, and other matters governed by these Rules.
- Participants may not conceal relevant information or misrepresent circumstances to obtain an advantage or avoid enforcement.
- Using another person, account, or intermediary to carry out deceptive conduct does not avoid responsibility.
7.5 — Confidentiality and Private Information
- Participants must respect the confidentiality of private tournament communications and information shared w ith them by Mathatised or another participant.
- Private communications in Team channels, administrative tickets, and other restricted channels should not be publicly shared or circulated without authorization.
- Information submitted privately during a disput e, investigation, or verification process should not be publicly disclosed while the matter is being reviewed.
- Participants must not share another participant’s private or personal information without authorization.
- An Administrator may instruct a particip ant to keep specific information confidential where necessary to protect an investigation, another participant, or tournament integrity.
- Deliberate disclosure of confidential information that disrupts an investigation, harms a participant, or undermines to urnament operations may result in disciplinary action.
7.6 — Illegal Conduct and Threats
- Participants must not engage in illegal conduct connected to MECS or its participants.
- This includes unlawful actions intended to interfere with tournament operations, harm another participant, obtain an unfair competitive advantage, or compromise another person’s security or account.
- Threats of violence, physical harm, or unlawful retaliation are prohibited.
- Conduct specifically prohibited elsewhere in these Rules rema ins subject to those provisions; this section does not replace them.
7.7 — Reporting Violations
- Any participant may report a suspected violation, regardless of whether they were directly involved.
- Reports must be submitted through the official Mathatised E sports Discord ticket system.
- Reports should include relevant information and evidence available to the reporting participant.
- Participants who make a report in good faith will not be subject to retaliation or discipline merely because the report is ultima tely not substantiated.
- Knowingly false, deliberately misleading, or malicious reports may result in disciplinary action.
- Participants must cooperate honestly with any resulting investigation or reasonable requests for additional information.
- The reporting-before-circulation requirement is governed by Section 5.3.
8. Enforcement & Disciplinary Measures
8.1 — Enforcement Authority
- Mathatised Esports and its authorized Tournament Administrators are responsible for enforcing these Rules.
- Administrators may investigate suspected violations and take action during or after a tournament.
- Only authorized Tournament Administrators may issue official disciplinary decisions.
8.2 — Available Disciplinary Measures
- Administrators may apply the follow ing penalties where permitted and appropriate: warning; game loss; match loss or forfeit; removal of a Player from a match or tournament; Team disqualification; loss of prize eligibility or prize; suspension from a future MECS tournament; permanent or exte nded ban from MECS.
- Any other corrective action may be used only where it is expressly authorized elsewhere in these Rules.
8.3 — Determining the Appropriate Penalty
- Administrators determine the appropriate permitted penalty based on the circumstances of t he violation.
- Relevant factors may include severity, intent, competitive impact, whether the conduct was repeated, cooperation, and other surrounding circumstances.
- Repeated, deliberate, or materially harmful violations may result in more severe penalties.
- No particular violation is required to receive an identical penalty in every circumstance.
8.4 — Individual and Team Responsibility
- A violation by an individual does not automatically result in punishment of the entire Team.
- Administrators determine wheth er the circumstances justify an individual penalty, Team penalty, or both.
- The decision may consider the severity of the violation, the Team’s involvement or benefit, and the effect on competition.
8.5 — Immediate Enforcement
- Administrators may take immediate action during an active tournament when necessary to protect competitive integrity, participants, or tournament operations.
- This may include removing a Player or Team from an active match or tournament where the action is within the permitted disciplinary measures.
- Administrators do not need to wait until a tournament has concluded to enforce these Rules.
- Material disciplinary decisions should be communicated to the affected participant or Team and recorded through official MECS communication where reasonably practicable.
8.6 — Investigations and Cooperation
- Participants must cooperate with reasonable requests from an authorized Tournament Administrator during an investigation.
- Participants must provide truthful and accurate information when requested.
- Administrators may request relevant evidence, records, screenshots, replays, recordings, account information, or other information reasonably necessary to investigate a suspected violation.
- Refusing to cooperate, intentionally withholding relevant information, or providing false or misleading information may result in disciplinary action under these Rules.
- Cooperation with an investigation does not prevent a participant from reporting a matter or submitting relevant evidence thr ough the official ticket process.

12. Appeals, Reviews & Final Decisions
12.1 — Request for Review
- A Player or Team affected by an administrative or disciplinary decision may submit a review request through the official Mathatised Esports Discord ticket system.
- The request should identify the decision and provide any genuinely relevant new information or evidence.
- Submitting a review request does not suspend the decision or require tournament operations to stop.
12.2 — Administrative Review
- An authorized Tournament Administrator may consider a review request where appropriate, particularly where genuinely new material information, evidence, or an administrative error is identified.
- A review request does not create a right to a different decision and does n ot require an Administrator to reconsider a matter solely because a participant disagrees with the original decision.
- Any corrective action must remain within the authority and penalties expressly provided by these Rules.
12.3 — Finality of Administrative Decisions
- Any decision made by an authorized Tournament Administrator is final and binding for MECS purposes.
- It does not matter which authorized Administrator makes the decision.
- A decision made by Wali Hassan (Mathatised), Ali Hassan, or another person f ormally designated and announced as a Tournament Administrator carries the same administrative authority.
- There is no requirement for another Administrator to approve, review, or confirm a decision before it takes effect.
- Any subsequent correction or chang e can occur only where an authorized Administrator determines that action is permitted and necessary under these Rules, such as to correct a material administrative error or address genuinely new material information.
12.4 — Tournament Continuity
- A review request does not automatically delay matches, bracket progression, or tournament operations.
- Unless an Administrator instructs otherwise, participants must continue following the published schedule and applicable administrative directions.
