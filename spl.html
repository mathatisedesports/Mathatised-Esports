Global participation

The experience

More than the results

The SPL was built to give teams a real series to play, not a quick knockout. Two formats, double elimination, and multiple weekends of matches against international opposition.

What the SPL involved

  • Competitive 3v3 and 2v2 formats
  • Double elimination in both tournaments
  • Multiple weekends of competition
  • International participation
  • Competitive matchups across the bracket
  • Broadcast and community coverage where available
