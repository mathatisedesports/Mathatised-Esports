How it works

One weekend, start to finish

Every MECS Regional follows the same simple path, so you always know what's coming.

  1. 01

    Register

    Sign your team up through the MECS registration process in our Discord.

  2. 02

    Eligibility check

    We check your two main players' combined peak MMR and confirm your spot.

  3. 03

    Saturday

    Upper quarterfinals and semifinals, plus the first lower-bracket rounds. All BO3, live on stream.

  4. 04

    Sunday

    Lower semifinal, BO5 upper and lower finals, then a BO7 Grand Final to crown the champion.

The format

Short, sharp and fully produced

Two days of high-level 2v2, with the same production and standards as every MECS event.

Eight teams

A tight field of eight teams per weekend, so every series matters from the very first match.

Double elimination

Lose once and you drop to the lower bracket with a second chance. Lose twice and you're out.

2v2, plus a sub

Two players in your main lineup and one optional substitute ready to step in.

Two days

Saturday and Sunday. Match times for each weekend are posted in our Discord.

Live and everywhere

Every match is livestreamed with casting on YouTube, Twitch and Kick, and the best moments become highlights and edits on YouTube Shorts, TikTok, Instagram and Facebook.

A title to your name

The winners become, for example, MECS APAC I Champion, and take the full $20 prize, paid in the first week of the following month.

Who can play

No rank requirement. Just combined MMR.

Any rank can enter. Instead, we add up the peak MMR of your two main players.

  • Each player's highest peak MMR across all formats (1v1, 2v2, 3v3…) counts
  • Your two main players need a combined 2,800 MMR or more
  • With the 50 MMR tolerance, a combined 2,750 still meets the requirement
  • Your substitute doesn't count towards the total
  • Accounts are checked through each player's Rocket League Tracker profile (Rules 3.3 and 3.13)
